About & background

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. My interests span machine learning, computer vision, reliable ML systems, and the quantitative methods behind them.

At IIT Hyderabad, I worked on estimating suspended solids in water from RGB images. My current research explores compositional generalization in vision and object-detection architecture.

Currently exploring Vision Transformers, representation learning, GPU computing, efficient inference, and distributed ML systems—with a long-term interest in AI research and operations research.

Quantitative foundations

Linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimization, numerical methods, and statistical learning. My applied work includes regression, regularization, gradient-based optimization, uncertainty estimation, and controlled benchmarking.
